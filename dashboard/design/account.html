your computers

Each computer has its own sync plan: what you tick here syncs to that computer only. The project list on the sync status page ticks for every computer you use at once. A computer's other settings belong to the computer, so they are changed from its tray (the CC icon, then Settings), and this page shows what it last reported.

your wired rigs

A wired rig works directly off the server share, so nothing is synced to it and it holds no ticks. It still reports in, and it can take fleet work.
