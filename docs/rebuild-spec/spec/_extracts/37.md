# Extract of inventory section 37

## Items

### 37-001: The Footage Sorter says it is loading, and says plainly when the first load fails.
- Where: footage-sorter-and-camera-cards / The sorter page
- What: Until the library has arrived the clip area shows a spinner with "Loading your clips" and "One moment.", and never "No clips match the current filters" during that first load. If the first load fails, an error message "The clips did not load: <reason>" stays up for 20 seconds and the clip area shows "The clips did not load", the reason and a Try again button that repeats the whole first load (clips, folder trees, calendar events and the missing-footage checks). Whenever the server refuses or fails a request, the person is shown the server's own explanation, or the first 200 characters of its answer, or the status it returned.

### 37-002: The Sorter chooses a phone or a desk layout before it first draws, and a person can force either.
- Where: footage-sorter-and-camera-cards / The sorter page
- What: A phone never sees the desk layout flash first. The phone layout is used when the device has a touch pointer that cannot hover and the smaller of screen width and window width is under 1024 px, or whenever the window is narrower than 720 px; a browser's "desktop site" request does not change this. A person can force the phone layout, the desk layout or automatic choice, and that choice is remembered on that browser until changed (automatic when the browser will not remember). The layout is re-decided shortly after a window stops being resized.
- Flag: differs: phone and desk show different controls, see 37-004 and 37-005

### 37-003: On a desk the Sorter shows sources, clips and a folders-and-details panel side by side.
- Where: footage-sorter-and-camera-cards / The sorter page
- What: The desk layout shows three areas at once: the sources (computers, their folders and cards), the clip grid, and a panel holding the destination folders, the calendar events, the selected clip's details, the audio worklist, the missing-footage checks and the crew's card tracker. A header carries totals and the main actions, a toolbar carries search, filters, sort and bulk actions, and a status line at the bottom reminds the person of the mouse and key moves ("click · ctrl+click · shift+click · drag box", "drag onto a destination folder to assign", "[ ] suggestion  Ctrl F search  Ctrl + Ctrl − size"), reading SORT normally and COPY while an open card shows a copy running. The sources area and the panel can be resized by dragging the border between them (sources between 150 and 560 px, the panel between 200 and 640 px); the sources area can be hidden so the clips get its space, and brought back. Widths, whether the sources are hidden, and the last panel opened are remembered on that browser.

### 37-004: On a phone the Sorter shows one of three views at a time, Source, Clips and Folders, switched from a bar that never moves.
- Where: footage-sorter-and-camera-cards / The sorter page
- What: The three views are Source, Clips (its button carries the current clip count) and Folders (the destination folders and every other panel). The phone opens on Clips. Leaving Clips adds one step to the browser history, so the phone's back gesture or button returns to Clips; moving between Source and Folders does not add more steps. Tapping a folder row in either tree filters the clips and jumps to Clips by itself; tapping the expand arrow, a button inside the row, a rename box or a count does not jump. The toolbar keeps the search box, the status buttons and a Filters button that opens and closes the other filters (camera, date, pick a day, kind, location, where, drive, sort and its direction, clear filters, thumbnail size, structure); the open filters scroll when taller than about half the screen. Accept suggestions appears only when it would do something. With a selection, the selection counter, Assign to… and Unassign appear. Controls are at least 44 px tall (small ones 36 px) on any touch device, form fields use text large enough that the phone does not zoom, the add, remove and rename buttons on folder rows are always visible (on a desk they appear on hover), dialogs fill the width, and messages stretch across the bottom above the bar.

### 37-010: Sizes, counts, durations, days and times read the same way everywhere in the Sorter.

### 37-012: Each clip card tells the person what the clip is, where it physically is and where it is headed.
- Where: footage-sorter-and-camera-cards / The sorter page
- What: A clip is one recording (a video, a still, an image sequence, an audio file or "other"), possibly several files. Its card shows the picture (or a placeholder for its kind until the picture arrives), the camera name ("other" when unknown), its kind when it is not video, its duration when known, a mark for where the footage physically is (on a card, in staging, in the final folders, or elsewhere) and whether a second copy exists, the state of a planned copy when there is one, the number of drives holding it (37-084), its name ("(unnamed)" when none), the recording time, the last part of its source folder and its size. The card shows whether it is selected, assigned or only suggested.

### 37-013: A clip that shares its file name with another is badged, saying which copy to keep and why.
- Where: footage-sorter-and-camera-cards / Telling recordings apart
- What: Of two clips with the same file name, the copy worth keeping gets a plain badge and the other an "S" badge. The rule, in order: the larger copy is kept; at the same size, the one already sorted into the destination; then the more recent recording; and when they are identical (same size and time) a fixed tie-break so the answer never changes between redraws. The tooltip names the rule. For the copy not kept: "A smaller copy of “<name>” - another clip has the same name and more of it", "Same size as the copy of “<name>” already sorted into the destination - that one is kept", "Same size as another copy of “<name>”, but recorded earlier - that one is kept", "Identical to another copy of “<name>” (same size, same time) - that one is kept; either would do". For the kept copy: "Duplicated: another clip has this name, and this is the larger copy to keep", "...this one is already sorted into the destination, so it is kept", "...this is the more recent recording", "...this one is kept so the answer does not move between renders".

### 37-018: Clips are selected with click, Ctrl or Cmd click, Shift click, a drawn box, or Ctrl or Cmd A.
- Where: footage-sorter-and-camera-cards / The sorter page
- What: A plain click selects only that clip and makes it the anchor. Ctrl/Cmd-click adds or removes one clip and moves the anchor. ### 37-021: Hovering a clip previews it, silently by scrubbing or with sound.
