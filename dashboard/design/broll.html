Mount paths (which local drive or volume each share resolves to) are set per editor in ~/.broll-companion.json on your own computer, not here. The broll share needs no entry: the CC Sync tray points it at the B-roll Archive in your synced tree.

CC Sync tray

"Send to Resolve" is built into the CC Sync tray, the app you already run. It listens on 127.0.0.1:8899 and drives DaVinci Resolve from there. If this says it is not running, start CC Sync (and if you still have the old separate BRoll Companion in your tray, quit it: it is retired and it holds this port).
