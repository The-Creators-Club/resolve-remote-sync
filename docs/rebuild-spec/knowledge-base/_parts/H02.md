### H02-001: A brief NAS hiccup blanked the whole accounts panel, including computers awaiting approval.
- What was going on: The accounts page asked the NAS and the sync server live every 30 seconds, and one failing source replaced the entire panel with an error.
- What the product must do: When one source behind a page fails, parts fed by other sources still show, and the failed part shows its last known state with its time.

- What the product must do: A server update that fails after its switch-over point restarts the dashboard on a known version and states which version is now running.

- What the product must do: Server installation waits for the NAS's install job to finish and reports success only when the job succeeded and the app is actually running.
