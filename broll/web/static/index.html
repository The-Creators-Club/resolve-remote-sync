Shares

Mount paths (which local drive/volume each share resolves to) are configured per-editor in ~/.broll-companion.json on your own machine, not here. The broll share needs no entry: your CC Sync companion points it at the B-roll Archive in your synced tree.

Companion app

checking…

"Send to Resolve" is built into the CC Sync companion — the tray app you already run. It listens on 127.0.0.1:8899 and drives DaVinci Resolve from there. If this says it isn't running, start the CC Sync companion (and if you still have the old separate BRoll Companion in your tray, quit it: it's retired and it holds this port).
