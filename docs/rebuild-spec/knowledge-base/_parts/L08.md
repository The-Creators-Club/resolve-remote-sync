### L08-001: A download was refused for "no space" by measuring the wrong disk
- What was going on: While the NAS share was briefly unmounted, the downloader measured the server's own small disk, kept that figure for a minute, and refused the next press as "free some space".
- What the product must do: A disk-space refusal is measured afresh after any refusal, and a share that has gone away is measured at most once a minute so clicks never hang.
