# Knowledge base part C06

## Entries

### C06-001: A field recorder file can peak above full scale and must not clip.
- What was going on: Recorder files in 32-bit float peaked at +1.8 and +2.2 dBFS; written to 24-bit as they were, every peak above 0 dBFS would have clipped.
- What the product must do: Recorder audio carried into an interview is brought below full scale before any fixed-point write, and no sample of the output clips.
- Kind: 1a
- Capability: cards-footage-in
- Found by: real use
- Date: 2026-09-13
- State: fixed and live
- Sources: C-ed-062186c1c

### C06-002: A dead recorder channel played in one ear.
- What was going on: Recorders often record one live channel; a dead one still peaked at -57 to -59 dBFS, and one click can give it a -18 dB peak, so peak tests misjudge it.
- What the product must do: A stereo interview with one live side is folded to its louder channel by average level, and a channel peaking under -30 dBFS counts as dead.

- Capability: cards-footage-in
- Found by: real use
- Date: 2026-10-09
- State: fixed and live
- Sources: C-ed-83c48d2e7, C-ed-1a1f57468
