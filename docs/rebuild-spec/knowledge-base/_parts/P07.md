# Knowledge base part P07

## Entries

### P07-001: Resolve hung on launch and scripting stayed dead until everything was restarted in order.
- What was going on: Resolve starts its scripting service 90-470 s into launch; any client connecting before Resolve registers with it kills scripting for that whole Resolve session, for every client.
- What the product must do: No client connects to Resolve's scripting until the service is listening and Resolve itself is connected to it; launching Resolve after the app never loses scripting.
- Kind: 1a
- Capability: resolve-on-the-editors-computer
- Found by: real use
- Date: 2026-08-21
- State: fixed and live
- Sources: P-01560, P-01563, P-01572, P-01573, P-01574, P-01575, P-01576

- What was going on: The danger window opens when Resolve's scripting service starts, not when Resolve starts, and a connect attempt with no service blocks about 4 s retrying.
- What the product must do: Readiness is judged from the service and Resolve's connection to it, never from a delay after the Resolve process appears, and polling never blindly attempts a connection.
