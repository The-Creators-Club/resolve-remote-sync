### cards-editor-1-Q1: Should every way of zooming the lane stop at the same limits, from the whole cut in to 400 px per second?
- Kind: built twice
- Today: the = and - keys zoom from 0.5 to 2000 px per second; the wheel, a trackpad pinch and the controller stop at 0.5 and 400; the lane itself says it zooms from the whole cut to 400 px per second. Zoomed fully out at 0.5 px per second, a long cut does not fit the window.
- Options: A: one range for every way of zooming, from the whole cut with a little air either side in to 400 px per second (about 13 px a frame). B: one range for every way, from the whole cut in to 2000 px per second. C: keep the keys reaching further than the other ways.
- Recommend: A, because one range is the fewest parts, 400 px per second already shows single frames, and "the whole cut" as the outer limit is what makes a 2.5-hour cut fit whole.
- Changes for a person: with A the keys stop zooming in sooner than they do now; with B the wheel and controller reach further in.
- Text assumes: A
- Items: 28-007, 28-058, 29-042
