### 31-004: "Exact words" searches every transcript of the episode, and the open cut list, instantly as the editor types.

Clicking one selects it, moves the playhead to its start if it is in the cut, and closes the sheet. Then "In the transcripts (<total>)" with result cards.
  - None found: "No transcript says that. Try fewer words, or search by meaning." Empty box: "Type to search every transcript of this episode, word for word. Results come in as you type."

  - In a card carrying both, original lines are the spoken words and English lines the gloss (a line counts as original when its share of non-Latin script is at least half that of the most original line).
  - English first: the English leads with the spoken words smaller under it. Original first: the reverse.
  - No English: the words lead and, on a card only (never the lane), the transcript paragraph's English under it is shown dimmed with a "¶" mark (tooltip "The transcript paragraph’s English for this stretch, not a translation of this cut.").
  - Shelf cards always show the English under the words, whatever the switch, two lines at most.
  - The words get the right language for fonts and line spacing (Chinese Simplified or Traditional, Japanese, Korean, Russian, Ukrainian), and the Cyrillic or CJK fonts the cut list needs are loaded once per script.
- Exact: yes

### 31-054: A card's English is taken from the transcript's own English and follows every trim at once.
- Where: cards-editor / The English a cut shows
- What: Order of preference: English the editor chose when inserting transcript words (kept as that card's English); the transcript's own English when the card covers whole paragraphs (at least 90% of each paragraph it touches, or missing less than 1 s of each); the transcript's English cut to the phrases the card keeps, phrase by phrase (tooltip "The transcript’s own English for these words, phrase by phrase (it follows every trim)."); else the dimmed paragraph hint. A stored English line that runs past its card (whole phrases from paragraphs the card barely reaches) gives way to the phrase-by-phrase English. A trimmed or split card's English appears in the same moment as the edit. The English is asked for about 0.3 s after the cut list changes and again when new word links or timed words arrive.
- Exact: yes

### 31-055: The lane shows each card's English, or says why there is none.

- What: The lane gets each card's English (never the "¶" hint).
