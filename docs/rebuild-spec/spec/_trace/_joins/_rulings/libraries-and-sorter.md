## Questions for the owner

### Q1: When an interview's camera and recorder cards are copied and sorted in the Footage Sorter, does that interview turn up in Timeline Cards and start processing by itself?
- Findings: footage-sorter-and-camera-cards-2 F1
- The two sides: Timeline Cards says footage brought in from camera and recorder cards reaches the episode on its own; the Footage Sorter files the clips into the episode's interviewee folder and never hands anything to Timeline Cards.
- Options: A: clips the Sorter files into an episode's interviewee folder are taken into that episode in Timeline Cards and processed with no further step. B: the Sorter files them, and a person drops the interview's clips onto the episode in Timeline Cards, as with any other footage.
- Recommend: B, because dropping clips onto the episode is already the one way in and a single gesture, while A adds a hand-over between two parts that nothing you have asked for needs.
- What you would notice: with A an interview starts processing in Timeline Cards as soon as its cards are sorted; with B it starts when someone drops its clips onto the episode.
