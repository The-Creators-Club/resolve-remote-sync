### D05-001: Resolve's scripting cannot move a clip, insert mid-timeline, or announce changes.
- What was going on: Resolve's scripting offers append and delete but no move, no mid-timeline insert and no event when a timeline changes; a change made in Resolve is only seen by looking again.
- What the product must do: A change the editor makes in Resolve shows in the cards within about one second, without Resolve announcing it.
- Kind: 1a
- Capability: cards-to-resolve-and-exports
- Found by: real use
- Date: 2026-08-09
- State: a standing rule
- Sources: D-tc-reorder-plan-001, D-tc-reorder-plan-005, D-tc-reorder-plan-006

### D05-002: Reading a 266-cut Resolve timeline takes about 150 ms.
- What was going on: Measured: listing every item on a 266-item track took about 90 ms, their source frames about 60 ms, the playhead 0.1 ms; appending in one batch cost about 11 ms per cut.
- What the product must do: Following a Resolve timeline of up to 500 cuts updates the cards within a second and never stalls the editor's own clicks in Resolve.
- Kind: 1a
- Capability: cards-to-resolve-and-exports
- Found by: real use
