### M04-001: Resolve's scripting cannot move a clip and announces no changes.
- What was going on: Resolve's scripting offers no move-clip call and no change events, so a timeline edited by hand can only be noticed by reading it again.
- What the product must do: Notice a change the editor makes by hand on the Resolve timeline within a few seconds, without relying on any notification from Resolve.
- Kind: 1a
- Capability: cards-to-resolve-and-exports
- Found by: real use
- Date: 2026-08-09
- State: a standing rule
- Sources: M-ed-reorder-tool-plan

### M04-002: A tab switch during an edit deleted from one timeline and placed onto another.
- What was going on: In Resolve's scripting, deleting acts on the named timeline but placing clips always lands on whichever timeline is open, so switching tabs mid-edit split the edit.
- What the product must do: Check before every write that the open timeline is the one being edited, and stop and roll back if it changed.
