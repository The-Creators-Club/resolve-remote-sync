# Extract of inventory section 01

## Items

### 01-001: Only one copy of the app runs on a computer, and starting a second one says where the first is.
- Where: editor-app / Starting, staying running and crashing
- What: Starting the app while it is already running does nothing except tell the person so. On Windows a message box titled with the product name reads "<product> is already running. Look for the <product> icon in your system tray (you may need to click the ^ arrow next to the clock)." On macOS an alert reads "<product> is already running." with "Look for the <product> icon in the menu bar." A copy left over from a crash or a restart never stops the app from starting, and when the check itself cannot work the app starts anyway. When the app replaces itself with a new version, the new copy waits up to about a minute and a half for the old one to close instead of refusing. On Windows the app shows the product's own icon on the taskbar.
- Flag: differs: Linux shows nothing when a second copy is refused.

### 01-002: On macOS the app lives only in the menu bar.
- Where: editor-app / The tray or menu-bar icon and its menu
- What: On macOS the app has no Dock icon, no application menu and no entry in the app switcher; it is reached only from its menu-bar icon. If that cannot be arranged it keeps a Dock icon and carries on.

### 01-003: An install that did not finish stops syncing until it is finished, and says how.
- Where: editor-app / Installing and setting up
- What: When the installer was interrupted before it finished, the app refuses to sync and says: "The last install of <product> on this computer did not finish, so this computer may have no <drive> drive and a half-written setup. Nothing will sync until it is finished. Run the <product> installer again and choose FINISH THE INSTALL." The drive is named from the studio's settings, else "your media drive". When the app cannot tell whether an install was interrupted, it does not refuse.

### 01-004: An app that dies without being asked to quit is started again by itself.
- Where: editor-app / Starting, staying running and crashing
- What: If the app on the editor's computer dies on its own (not after Quit, not when someone ends it by hand, not while it updates itself), it is started again automatically, a limited number of times an hour. A death nobody asked for is recorded so the next start knows the last run did not end cleanly.
- Flag: undetermined: how many relaunches an hour are allowed is set elsewhere.

### 01-005: Parts of the app that stop working are restarted by the app itself, within limits, and repeated restarts are shown.
- Where: editor-app / Starting, staying running and crashing
- What: When the part of the app that watches the editing program, reads its media pool, or works through this computer's projects stops or stops making progress for more than about 30 minutes (longer for project syncing when its turn per project is long), the app restarts that part by itself, checking about once a minute. The first restart is at once; repeat restarts of the same part wait longer each time (one minute, doubling, at most ten minutes), and after six in an hour the app stops trying ("this needs a human, not another restart") until the hour has passed. It never restarts anything while the app is shutting down or while a window or a media copy-in is open that a restart would kill. Three restarts of one part within an hour put a line in the app's menu telling the editor. The restarts are remembered across app restarts, kept for 24 hours, and the admin sees per part how many in the last 24 hours and the last hour, when, and the last error.

### 01-006: The app keeps a log of bounded size that the editor can be asked to send.
- Where: editor-app / Settings, records and background work
- What: The app keeps a log on the computer, at most about 55 MB in total (older entries are dropped). How much detail it records is a setting on that computer. If the configured place for the log cannot be used, the app logs to its usual place instead, records which setting was wrong, and still starts; if even that fails it starts without a log.

### 01-007: At start the app says at once if this computer will not sync, and confirms a finished update.
- Where: editor-app / Telling the editor what is wrong
- What: Shortly after starting the app shows one of: "NOT SYNCING: <product> isn't fully set up on this computer. <how to get help>." when a setting is missing or wrong; otherwise, when the licence is not accepted, "NOT SYNCING: <licence sentence>" and the licence is offered (01-012); and on the first start after an update, about three seconds in, "Update complete. Now running v<version>." If the tray or menu-bar icon cannot be shown, the app still runs and syncs without it.

### 01-008: A computer whose set-up is incomplete names the broken setting in plain words.
- Where: editor-app / Telling the editor what is wrong
- What: The worst set-up problem is shown as one sentence saying what is missing and what to set. A heading "WHAT IS NOT SET UP" lists up to three problems, worst first. While a problem stands, the sync status reads "NOT SYNCING: this computer isn't fully set up -- <first problem, or 'see the log'>".

### 01-009: Notifications appear at the app's icon under the studio's name, and only one app window is open at a time.
- Where: editor-app / Windows, dialogs and notifications
- What: Every notification is shown at the tray or menu-bar icon, titled with the studio's brand (read at the moment it is shown); with no icon nothing is shown. The app never opens two of its own windows at once: a window that would open while another is up waits and opens the moment the first closes, one at a time and without repeating what was already shown; some warnings that are not urgent are skipped and asked again later instead.

### 01-010: An editor signs in to the app with their username and password, and nothing syncs or reports until they do.
- Where: editor-app / Signing in, the computer's identity and the licence
- What: Signing in checks the username and password with the server. On success the app starts syncing if nothing else stops it, and takes any update the server offered in its answer (including the reason the server is holding a build back). Signing out stops syncing and stops this computer reporting to the dashboard; the sync status reads 'sign in required -- use the menu's "Sign in..." to authenticate before syncing'. Requiring sign-in is a setting on the computer, on by default; when it is off, the computer reports under a name set on the computer, and with no name set it does not report at all. Resuming from pause while nobody is signed in says "You're not signed in, so nothing will sync. Right-click the tray icon → Sign in…" and starts nothing. "Sign in again" re-runs the sign-in while already signed in, for when the server has withdrawn a sign-in that still looks valid on the computer.

### 01-011: When a sign-in expires, syncing stops and the editor is told why, including when the cause is a wrong clock.
- Where: editor-app / Signing in, the computer's identity and the licence
- What: About once a minute (a setting on the computer) the app checks that its sign-in is still valid. When it becomes invalid, syncing stops (if sign-in is required) and the editor is told either "This computer's clock is <N ahead of/behind> the server's, which is why your <product> sign-in looks expired. Fix the clock (Windows: Date and time -> Sync now) and syncing will start again." when the computer's clock is far off the server's, or otherwise "Your <product> sign-in has expired, so syncing has stopped. Right-click the tray icon → Sign in…". When it becomes valid again, syncing starts by itself.

### 01-012: Nothing syncs on a computer until someone there accepts the current licence, which the app offers by itself.
- Where: accounts-privacy-legal / The licence a person accepts
- What: Until someone on this computer has accepted the current licence version, nothing syncs (everything else in the app still runs). About three seconds after start the app opens the licence window, and offers it again every minute until it has actually been shown once, waiting while another app window is open; if the desktop is not ready yet it tries again a minute later. The window reads "This computer is NOT SYNCING until someone here accepts the <product> licence agreement (version <V>). Read it below. Accepting records your agreement on this computer only, and syncing starts straight away." followed by the licence text, shown without authoring marks (the text accepted and recorded is the unaltered licence). Accepting starts syncing at once (subject to everything else) and says "Licence accepted, syncing is starting."; if the acceptance cannot be saved: "Couldn't save your acceptance. Check disk space and try again." Declining or closing it says "NOT SYNCING: the licence agreement was not accepted. <how to accept it>." and stops the automatic offer; "Show the licence again" in the menu opens it any number of times while there is something to accept. A build that lacks its licence text says "NOT SYNCING: this build is missing its licence document. <how to get help>." and does not retry. If checking the licence fails with an error, the licence counts as accepted. The accepted licence record is sent to the dashboard.

### 01-013: The editor can restart the app from its settings.
- Where: editor-app / Starting, staying running and crashing
- What: "RESTART NOW" restarts the app in place. It does nothing when work a restart would kill is under way (an app window open, media being copied in) or when the restart cannot be done.

### 01-014: The editor can copy a full description of the computer's state and send it to the admin with one button.
- Where: editor-app / Telling the editor what is wrong
- What: "Copy diagnostics" copies a plain-text description to the clipboard, sends it to the dashboard for the admin, and says "Diagnostics copied, and sent to your admin's dashboard." When another app window is open it says "Another <product> window is open, so the diagnostics went to the log and to your admin's dashboard instead of the clipboard."; when the clipboard cannot be used, "Couldn't reach the clipboard. The diagnostics were written to the log and sent to your admin's dashboard." Nothing is sent without a signed-in person (or the computer's set name when sign-in is not required); a failed send is not reported. The description covers: the time, the app version, the operating system and whether it is an installed build; the signed-in person ("NOT SIGNED IN") and when the sign-in expires; whether syncing is on, paused, signed-in, started; the sync folder and the sync drive's state (including whether macOS is blocking access); where the server is; the editing program's open project and whether the app can talk to the editing program ("never polled this session", "connected", or "NOT CONNECTED -- <reason>"); the counts of clips outside the shared tree, on a wrong drive, missing, skipped for now and skipped ever, and the last scan time; update history (attempts, failures, "GIVEN UP on that build: it kept crashing and was rolled back (do not install it by hand)", "GIVEN UP on that build: it has failed N times", "rolled back automatically from v<X> (it kept crashing)", "REFUSING v<X> since <time>: <reason>"); every set-up problem and warning; which projects are being synced; shared-work state (taking work or not, the job held, idle time, where unknown means "not idle"); every kind of syncing's full status; whether project-file sharing answers right now and whether any peers are "NOT on a direct path"; the last accepted report ("NEVER (nothing this computer has sent was accepted)"), failures in a row and the clock difference with the server ("+Ns (this computer is AHEAD/BEHIND)" or "not measured yet"); recent crashes; recent self-restarts (01-005); and the last 40 lines of the log. Any part that cannot be read says so in its line and the rest still appears.
- Flag: several ways in: the editor's button, an automatic send when syncing falls into an error, and the admin's request (01-015).

### 01-015: The admin receives a computer's diagnostics automatically when its syncing falls into an error, and on request.
- Where: dashboard / Reports from computers
- What: When any kind of syncing on a computer newly falls into an error (not while it stays in error), the computer sends its diagnostics (01-014) to the dashboard, at most once per kind of syncing per hour. An admin can ask a computer "why" from the dashboard; the computer answers with its diagnostics once per request, at its next report, one request at a time; a failed send is tried again on a later report after about five minutes.

### 01-016: Diagnostics hide project names and paths when this computer or the studio has switched off reporting about them.
- Where: accounts-privacy-legal / What the product records about a person
- What: If the computer or the studio has withheld reporting of the editing program's project, of the files on the computer, or of the media pool, the diagnostics replace every known project name with "<project>" and every path in the studio's folders with "<root>/<file>", and end with a note naming what was withheld. If hiding fails, the whole description is replaced by a one-line notice that it was withheld.

### 01-017: Each computer tells the dashboard, about once a minute, what it is doing and what is wrong.
- Where: dashboard / Reports from computers
- What: While someone is signed in, each computer reports to the dashboard about every minute (never more often than every 30 seconds). The admin can learn from it: what each kind of syncing is doing and on which project (named as the dashboard names it, a borrowed folder under the project borrowing it); the queue of projects; the project open in the editing program and its media pool (each clip's bin, name, path, whether it is a proxy, an original or other, and whether the file is on this computer, a file seen present re-checked every 15 minutes); which files are on this computer, file by file only for projects ticked here; wired or remote; the computer's identity for project-file sharing; the editor; whether sharing reaches each peer directly; proxy coverage; YouTube clips downloaded but not yet in the editing program; b-roll and music indexing progress; what shared work the computer can take and how long nobody has used it; the safety stops; free space on the sync drive and the system drive; the clock difference with the server; whether the dashboard is accepting its reports; update attempts and refusals; whether the local send-to-editing-program service works; the sync drive's state; the editing program's health (01-041); project folders that have disappeared since last time and folders in no plan (up to 20 each); project folders held back because their list of what to share never arrived (up to 20); conflict copies of project files held here (reported only); recent self-restarts; overridden project removals (the last 5); staging space held by indexing; and the one-sentence "why is this computer not syncing" (01-018). Any list of clips is capped at 50. A value nobody has measured yet is sent as unknown, never as zero. One part failing never removes the others.

### 01-018: The dashboard and the app show one sentence saying why a computer is not syncing, and since when.
- Where: sync / Saying truthfully what the sync is doing
- What: The first reason that applies, in this order, is shown with the time it began (or the first time it was seen):
  1. "Nobody is signed in to <product> on this computer, so nothing is syncing"
  2. the licence sentence
  3. "This computer's clock is <N ahead of/behind> the server's, so proxy download will not transfer anything"
  4. to 6. the sync drive is absent, not answering, or mounted in the wrong place (the sentences in 01-024)
  7. "Not downloading proxies: <reason or 'this drive is nearly full'>"
  8. and 9. the studio-wide stop, then this computer's stop (01-022)
  10. "Syncing is paused from this computer's tray"
  11. "Proxy download is stopped as a safety measure: <reason>"
  12. "No projects are ticked for this computer[ (<detail>)]"
  13. "Your project folder for <name> is not where <product> expects it. Did you rename or move it?[ (<N> project folders are missing)]"
  14. "<N projects> are not sharing yet - waiting for their filter list: <up to 3 projects>"
  15. "<what> stopped making progress for <N minutes> and was restarted" (ignored once it has recovered or after a while)
  16. "Project-file sharing is not running on this computer, so project files are not being shared" (not on a computer that syncs nothing)
  17. "This computer cannot reach the server: <reason, or 'upload and proxy download are both failing'>"
  Nothing applying means the computer is syncing.
- Flag: differs: the order in which the app itself decides not to start syncing (01-019) is different from this order.

### 01-019: Syncing does not start for six reasons, each shown on the sync status in its own words.
- Where: sync / Studio-wide stops and the editor's pause
- What: In this order: paused by the editor ("Syncing is paused from the tray."); stopped on this computer or studio-wide (01-022); a set-up problem (its sentence, else "This computer is not set up for syncing yet."); licence not accepted; the sync drive absent ("PAUSED: <drive> is disconnected -- plug it back in and syncing resumes on its own", the drive named from the studio's settings); and a computer set to sync nothing ("This computer works straight off the server, so there is nothing to sync.", status "sync disabled: this computer works directly off the server"). A computer with proxy download switched off shows that proxy download is switched off on this computer because it uses the NAS directly.
- Flag: differs: see 01-018.

### 01-020: The editor can pause and resume syncing from the menu; a pause lasts until resumed or the app restarts.
- Where: sync / Studio-wide stops and the editor's pause
- What: Pause stops uploads and proxy downloads, including the immediate upload of new files; small project files keep sharing during a pause. Resume restarts them and fetches any originals requested meanwhile. The pause is not remembered: restarting the app resumes syncing. On a computer set to sync nothing, the toggle changes nothing else. Unplugging the drive or a stop does not change the pause, and a returning drive never undoes it.

### 01-021: "Sync now" checks the server for changes at once and says what will run.
- Where: sync / Saying truthfully what the sync is doing
- What: Refused with the reason from 01-019 if one applies. When the dashboard's plan for this computer is known and empty: "Nothing is ticked for this computer, so there is nothing to sync." Otherwise: "Checking the server for changes now." with which kinds of syncing will run. If it cannot start: "<product> could not start a sync pass. <open the log and send it>."

### 01-022: The editor can stop all syncing on their computer, and an admin can stop it for every computer; a stop survives a restart.
- Where: sync / Studio-wide stops and the editor's pause
- What: "Stop syncing" stops everything on this computer: uploads, proxy downloads and project-file sharing, and it stays stopped across restarts until released. The editor is told "SYNCING IS STOPPED on this computer.\n<reason>\nNothing is uploading, downloading or sharing until it is started again." and the status reads "STOPPED: syncing is STOPPED on this computer[ -- <reason>]". An admin's studio-wide stop reaches each computer at its next report and reads "STOPPED: your administrator stopped syncing for the whole fleet[ -- <reason>]"; it cannot be released from the editor's computer, only by the admin. When the admin releases it, syncing starts (subject to everything else) and the editor is told "Your administrator started syncing again." Releasing a stop leaves held back any project still waiting for its list of what to share. A stop touches only the sharing this product set up, never other programs' on the same computer. While stopped, or paused, or on a computer set to sync nothing, the product does not try to revive its sharing when it is down.

### 01-023: Whether a computer is wired or remote, and whether it syncs at all, is set on that computer alone.
- Where: sync / Wired or remote
- What: The computer's own setting decides whether it is wired (works straight off the server, syncs nothing) or remote; whether the signed-in person is an admin plays no part, and signing in or out cannot change it. The choice is reported to the dashboard. Whether popups about clips outside the tree are shown is a separate setting not changed by this.
- Flag: undetermined: whether a wired computer also has proxy download switched off is decided elsewhere.

### 01-024: When the sync drive goes away, everything that writes to it stops and the editor is told which way it went.
- Where: sync / The sync drive
- What: The app notices the sync folder disappearing (unplugged), the drive no longer answering though plugged in, or (macOS) the drive mounted at a numbered place such as "/Volumes/Name 1". When it cannot tell, it assumes the drive is there. On the first change of an outage: if work was owed, the owed-work warning starts (01-026); otherwise one notification: "Sync paused: <Drive> is not answering - reconnect it or restart this computer." (with a repeating reminder while it stays so), "Sync paused: <Drive> is mounted at the wrong place. See the <product> window for what to do.", or "Sync paused: <drive> is disconnected." A change of kind within the same outage says the matching sentence again. All uploading, downloading and copying into the tree stops; the editor's own pause is not touched. Immediate upload of new files pauses too. When the drive comes back the app says "Drive reconnected, sync resumed." (01-025); a drive present at start says nothing.

### 01-025: A drive mounted at the wrong place on macOS gets a dialog with three steps to fix it.
- Where: sync / The sync drive
- What: Once per outage the editor is told "<Drive> is mounted at the wrong path. Sync is paused until it is fixed. See the <product> window." and a dialog (title "... drive mounted at the wrong path", button OK) explains that an empty leftover folder sits where the drive should be, and says: eject the drive; delete the leftover empty folder; plug it back in and check it appears at the right place. If another app window is open, only the notification is shown.

### 01-026: A drive that returns is not consent to sync: syncing resumes only if nothing else stops it, and catches up at once.
- Where: sync / The sync drive
- What: When the drive returns, nothing resumes if the editor paused, if there is a set-up problem, or if sign-in is required and nobody is signed in. Otherwise syncing resumes, immediate upload is resumed, and piled-up work is checked at once rather than at the next turn.

### 01-027: A sync drive that is missing at start is waited for, not treated as a broken install.
- Where: sync / The sync drive
- What: When the only problem at start is that the sync folder does not exist and it lives on a removable drive, the app waits and starts syncing by itself when the drive appears. A sync folder that is not set at all is still a set-up problem.

### 01-028: A drive pulled while work was owed gets a warning naming what is owed, then reminders until it is back.
- Where: sync / What is owed
- What: Only work actually moving counts as owed (a kind of syncing that says "syncing" but moves nothing does not). The warning names it, for example "2 uploads (2.3 GB left)", and a reminder follows every so many minutes (a setting on the computer; 0 means the first warning only). The episode is remembered across restarts and only the drive coming back ends it; on return the reminders stop. If the previous run left such an episode unfinished, its reminders resume.

### 01-029: After an update on macOS, the app says when macOS has stopped it reading the sync drive.
- Where: editor-app / Security warnings, permissions and certificates
- What: Once, shortly after starting a newly updated version on macOS, if macOS blocks the app from reading the sync folder, the editor is told: "macOS is blocking access to the sync volume after the update. Re-grant <product> Full Disk Access in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Full Disk Access." It also appears in the diagnostics.
- Flag: differs: macOS only.

### 01-030: Files on the editor's computer that are not on the server are moved aside, never deleted, and the editor can open where they went.
- Where: sync / Deletions and removing a project
- What: When downloading would remove local files the server does not have, they are moved into a recovery folder inside the sync folder and the editor is told: "Some files in your project folder weren't on the server, so <product> moved them (never deleted) to:\n<folder>\nCopy anything you still need back out of there." (title "... files moved to the recovery folder"). "OPEN THE RECOVERY FOLDER" opens it, offered only when it exists. The app shows where it is, how many batches, total size, the oldest batch, and how many days copies are kept (a setting on the computer). A file the server moved into a project this computer does not sync stays in the recovery folder.

### 01-031: Proxy download stops itself as a safety measure, and only a person restarts it, from the menu or the dashboard.
- Where: sync / Safety stops
- What: When the safety stop trips, the editor is told once: "<product> STOPPED downloading proxies as a safety measure:\n<reason>\nWhen your admin says the server is fine, use the tray's "Resume proxy download"." (title "... proxy download stopped"). "Resume proxy download" clears both the safety stop and a full-disk pause; with neither set it says "proxy download is not stopped", on error "could not resume proxy download -- see the log", on success "Proxy download resumed." and downloading starts at once. An admin can resume it from the dashboard; the computer applies that request once only, at its next report, and the editor is told "<admin> checked the server and started proxy download again." (title "... proxy download resumed"). A later trip needs a fresh click. The stop is remembered across restarts and survives clearing the app's working records.
- Flag: several ways in: the editor's menu and the admin's dashboard.

### 01-032: Proxy download pauses by itself when the sync drive is nearly full and resumes when space is freed.
- Where: sync / Disk space
- What: The editor is told once: "<product> is not downloading proxies:\n<reason>\nYour uploads are still running. Free up some space and it starts again on its own." (title "... proxy download paused"). The pause is remembered across restarts.

### 01-033: The editor is shown files that will never upload, shared folders that do not work here, and projects renamed on the server.
- Where: sync / Files that must never sync
- What: The app shows: files dated in the future (a camera clock set wrong), which are never uploaded; up to 5 files whose name already exists on the server at a different size (so this computer's version will never upload), with both sizes; up to 10 sentences about shared or borrowed folders that are not working on this computer (commonly the LUT library); and up to 10 projects an admin renamed on the server whose folder this computer moved to match (old name, new, when, whether the editing program was relinked). Only projects ticked here count; warnings about projects no longer ticked are dropped. If the app cannot tell what is ticked, it drops nothing. When the file-watching for new uploads stops or recovers because the drive stopped answering, the editor is told.

### 01-034: When a ticked project's folder has been moved on the computer, a button puts it back.
- Where: sync / Project folders on an editor's computer
- What: When the app finds a ticked project's folder somewhere other than expected, it says so (01-018) and offers a button to move it back; it never does it by itself, never overwrites an existing folder and never deletes. Messages: "<product> could not check where that folder is. See the log."; "<product> cannot find that project folder anywhere on this computer. If you moved it to another drive, move it back by hand."; "<product> is still starting up. Try again in a moment."; "<product> could not move that folder back. Close Resolve and Explorer on it and try again."; "<N folders> put back; <M> could not be moved. Close Resolve and Explorer on them and try again."; "<N project folders> put back where <product> expects them. Syncing starts again on the next pass."

### 01-035: An editor can remove a project from their computer, and is stopped while it still holds work the server lacks.
- Where: sync / Deletions and removing a project
- What: The editor's settings list each project ticked for this computer (marked when upload only) with "REMOVE '<project>'". None are listed on a wired computer or with no known plan. Before deleting, the app fetches the latest plan and checks, refusing when a check cannot answer:
  - not ticked here: "that project is not ticked for this computer";
  - part of it is shared into another project still ticked here: "part of this project ('<sub>') is shared into <other>, which is still selected here";
  - not uploaded yet: "<N video files> have not been uploaded yet (e.g. a, b, c)" ("files" for an upload-only project), or "<product> could not reach the server to check whether your footage has been uploaded";
  - changed after upload: "<N files> changed after they were uploaded, and a changed file does not upload again on an upload-only project (e.g. ...). Rename them first, so they upload as new files", or that it could not check;
  - same name, different size on the server: "<N files> share a name with a different file already on the server, so they will never upload (e.g. ...). Rename them first", or that it could not check;
  - files no sync carries: "<N files> in this folder are not carried by any sync, so the server will never have them (e.g. ...)", or "<product> could not read this project's folder to check what is in it";
  - dated in the future: "<N files> are dated in the future (a camera clock set wrong), which <product> will not upload (e.g. ...). One a few hours ahead uploads by itself once that time passes; otherwise its modified date has to be reset to now (ask whoever looks after your computers) before it can upload";
  - project files not fully shared (not for upload-only): "the shared files for this project are only <P>% synced to the server", "<N shared files> have not arrived on this computer yet", or that it could not ask.
  Blocked: "'<project>' still has work that has not reached the server, so nothing was deleted:" with each reason, then "Leave <product> running until it is finished, then try again." The editor may override by typing the project's name; the override (project, time, how much was pending, up to 4 reasons) is reported to the admin. Other refusals: on a wired computer ("its tree IS the server tree"), with the drive out ("<drive> is disconnected, so nothing was removed. Plug it back in and try again."), and for an unsafe or out-of-place path ("nothing was deleted"). Then, stopping before any deletion on failure: untick it on the dashboard ("could not untick on the dashboard (<message>) -- nothing was deleted"); stop sharing it here ("the project was unticked, but its shared files could not be removed from this computer -- nothing was deleted. Try again in a minute."); delete the folder ("'<project>' is no longer synced here (its folder was already gone)"; "'<project>' was unticked and unshared, but some files could not be deleted (<error>). Delete the folder by hand -- it is safe now."). Success: "'<project>' removed from this computer. The server copy is untouched."

### 01-036: The computer stays awake while it is moving data, and Windows warns at shutdown.
- Where: sync / Uploads that cannot resume, shutdown and sleep
- What: While something is actually moving (an upload or download transferring, project files with a connected peer to share with, a b-roll batch being indexed, a proxy being made), the computer is kept from sleeping when idle (a setting, on by default). On Windows the shutdown screen shows "still syncing" with the reason (a setting, on by default). Neither applies while paused, with a set-up problem, or with the drive out. On macOS a log-out closes the app cleanly. A Windows shutdown counts as a clean exit, not a crash.

### 01-037: Half-copied files left by an interrupted copy are reported at start and never deleted.
- Where: proxies-and-file-moves / Writing, copying and deleting files safely
- What: At start the app looks through the tree once. Half-copied files from an interrupted copy-in are reported to the editor: "Found <N half-copied files> from an interrupted copy. Nothing was deleted. <how to get help>." Empty placeholder files an interrupted copy left are removed quietly. Half-made proxy files from an interrupted encode are left alone and nobody is told.

Otherwise "Installing v<version>…", then it downloads, checks, swaps and restarts; on failure "Update failed. You're still on v<current>, nothing is broken. <how to get help>." The previous build is kept until the new one has proved healthy (an accepted report, or about an hour when the dashboard cannot be reached).

### 01-077: Failed updates are tried again less and less often, and a build that keeps failing is given up on.
- Where: releases-and-updates / Installing an update on an editor's computer
- What: After a failed attempt the next unattended one waits longer (roughly 10 minutes, then an hour, then 6 hours); after too many failures at one version unattended tries stop, and the menu and the dashboard say so. A build this computer went back from because it kept crashing is never installed unattended again; only a different version or the editor's own click moves it on (and once it has moved past it, its failures are forgotten). These survive restarts. An offer the computer will not install (for example below the signed minimum) waits on the same curve, does not count as a failure, and the refusal and its reason are reported.
- Flag: undetermined: the maximum attempts and exact waits are set elsewhere.

### 01-078: An admin can push an update to one computer, which installs it like the editor's click.
- Where: releases-and-updates / Pushing updates and seeing who has what
- What: When an admin presses update for this computer, it installs at its next report, only if that exact version is the build it is already offered; otherwise the request is ignored and nobody is told. A version already running is not reinstalled. The editor is told "Your administrator is updating <product> to v<X>." The same waits and refusals apply as for the editor's click, retried quietly (about 90 seconds after waiting for open work, 10 minutes with no offer, otherwise the failure waits); a new request for the same version is tried at once. It can never install anything but the signed offer, nor go below the signed minimum.

### 01-079: A studio can let its computers take newer builds without anyone clicking.
- Where: releases-and-updates / Offering an update to a computer
- What: A studio setting, off by default (and treated as off when the studio's settings cannot be read): when on, a NEWER offered build installs by itself; an older build is never taken unattended and stays in the menu. It keeps trying while that version is offered, under the same rules and waits, stopping when the offer is withdrawn or replaced or the setting is turned off. Refusal notices show only on the first try.
- Flag: dormant

### 01-080: A build that keeps crashing at start is replaced by the previous one automatically, and the editor is told.
- Where: releases-and-updates / Rolling back and withdrawing a build
- What: If a newly installed build keeps crashing at start (by its notes, three times in ten minutes), the app puts the previous build back before starting anything. It cannot when there is no previous copy, the previous version is unknown, or going back would go below the signed minimum; then it carries on. After a rollback, about three seconds after start: "The last update kept crashing, so <product> went back to v<version>." The dashboard is told which build it went back from until a report is accepted.
- Flag: undetermined: the crash window is set elsewhere.

### 01-081: Quitting closes the app cleanly and hands back work in progress.
- Where: editor-app / Starting, staying running and crashing
- What: Quit stops everything: a shared job goes back to the other computers, b-roll indexing resumes from its last checkpoint at the next start, the owed-drive reminder is kept, and the next start does not report a crash. On macOS, if a window is still stuck ten seconds later, the app ends itself so it can be started again.

### 01-082: What a computer withholds from its reports follows the editor's switches and the studio's.
- Where: accounts-privacy-legal / What the product records about a person
- What: What is withheld at any time is everything withheld by this computer or by the studio. Withholding the editing program's project, the files on this computer or the media pool empties the media-pool report, blanks those fields in what the computer can offer, and hides project names in recorded clip-path changes and their answers. The work itself (relinking, checks) still runs.

### 01-083: Crashes are recorded on the computer; sending them to an outside service exists but is off.
- Where: editor-app / Starting, staying running and crashing
- What: Every unexpected error is written to a crash record on the computer with the end of the log, and a hard crash leaves a record too; recent ones appear in diagnostics and are counted for the admin. Sending crash reports to an outside service can be set on a computer but cannot work in the shipped build.
- Flag: dormant

### 01-084: A field backup page can be opened from the app's menu.
- Where: editor-app / The tray or menu-bar icon and its menu
- What: When field backup is on for a computer, "Field backup..." opens a local web page. If it cannot take its port, only that page is lost.
- Flag: undetermined: whether field backup is on by default.

### 01-085: Camera-card reporting from an editor's computer is off unless switched on for that computer.
- Where: footage-sorter-and-camera-cards / Recognising cards and drives
- What: The editor's computer reports the camera cards and drives it sees, and records copies made, to the Footage Sorter only when this is switched on for that computer.
- Flag: dormant

### 01-086: Settings on the editor's computer, by what they do.
- Where: editor-app / Settings, records and background work
- What: Each is a setting on that computer; its default in brackets:
  - require sign-in before syncing (on); the name to report under when sign-in is not required (none);
  - wired or remote (remote); sync at all (on; off on a wired computer);
  - proxy download (on);
  - popups for clips outside the tree (on); how long a shown clip stays quiet (5 minutes);
  - how often the open timeline is read (about every 3 seconds) and the media pool (about every 2 minutes);
  - the scripting-dead warning (on) and its interval (5 minutes);
  - restart itself when the editing program quits (on);
  - attach synced proxies (on);
  - projects to ignore (none);
  - keep the computer awake while syncing (on); warn at Windows shutdown (on);
  - days to keep recovery copies; minutes between owed-drive reminders (0 means only the first warning);
  - how often to check the LUT and stills links (about 15 minutes); LUT link and stills each on or off;
  - the server location for grading (worked out from the dashboard's address; can be switched off);
  - how often the sign-in is checked (about a minute);
  - how much detail the log keeps;
  - rehearse copy-ins without copying (off);
  - the dashboard address (none means the whole tree syncs, 01-042).
- Flag: undetermined: who may change each one is not stated; only the shared-work settings can be changed by an admin from the dashboard (01-067).
