### cards-editor-1-U2
- Answer: After any edge edit (dragging a clip edge in the lane, the pad's bumpers, a hold on the phone, the trim and extend keys), the playhead stays on the same spoken moment of the clip it was in. A trim earlier on the timeline makes the playhead slide back with that moment. If the trim cut away the very moment the playhead was on, it lands on the last frame of that same clip, never on the next clip's first frame. A playhead in a gap keeps its distance into that gap, and a playhead past the end keeps its time. An edit never carries the playhead forward: when a ripple extend adds material before it, the playhead stays where it is on screen. None of this applies while playing: the playhead is only put back when playback is stopped.

### cards-editor-2-U1
- Answer: A cut list counts as open while the server holds it open. The server lets it go once no device is connected to it and nothing has touched it for 10 minutes. "Touched" means a device sending an edit, joining, leaving, or reading the list. Edits are not the only thing that counts. The idle check runs on the 15-second heartbeat, so the real wait is 10 minutes to about 10 minutes 15 seconds after the last device left or acted. The time can be changed on the server, with 10 minutes as the default. If the server holding it has died, its hold goes stale after 30 seconds without a heartbeat.
- Sure: read in the code
- Where: `editor/features/projects/server.ts` 451-481 (`/api/projects/rename`, `ProjectLock.acquire`, the refusal sentence at 479); `editor/server/sync/options.ts` 46-48, 68 (`idleCloseMs: 10 * 60_000`, `TC_SYNC_IDLE_MS`, `lockBeatMs` 10 s, `lockStaleMs` 30 s); `editor/server/sync/service.ts` 234-251 (idle close: `s.clients === 0 && now - s.lastActivity > idleCloseMs`, checked every `min(pingMs, 15 s)`); `editor/server/sync/store.ts` 212, 510, 709, 731, 738 (`lastActivity` set on submit, join, leave, snapshot read); `editor/core/src/protocol.ts` 134 (`pingMs: 15_000`).
