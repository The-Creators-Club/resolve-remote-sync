### D03-001: The owner prefers the product's own transcription to Resolve's built-in one
- What was going on: The owner ruled the product's own word-timed transcription "is preferable to Resolve['s], much preferable, it's more accurate", for interviews as well as montage clips.
- What the product must do: For every clip, word timing comes from the product's own transcription first and Resolve's transcription only when none exists, and each cut's source is shown.
